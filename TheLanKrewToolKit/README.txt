The Lan Krew ToolKit, for World of Warcraft: Forever

INSTALL
1. Quit the game.
2. Extract the zip into the Interface\AddOns folder of your Forever install, for example
   World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns
   That leaves two folders there: ByteTerrace_Core and TheLanKrewToolKit.
   To update, delete both old folders first, but save your sounds in TheLanKrewToolKit\Media.
3. Start the game. At character select, AddOns lists The Lan Krew ToolKit, switched on.

GETTING AROUND
  /tlk            settings (also under Esc > Options > AddOns, but with a controller, use /tlk)
  /tlk help       every command
  /tlk sound      the sounds you can add. The addon ships none (see Media\README.txt)
  /tlk journal    this session's highlights
  /tlk krew       who in your group runs the toolkit
  /tlk ledger     the Warband's marks, grudges, champions, and wars
  /tlk rally      calls the Krew to you
  /tlk read       reads the open quest aloud (Scholar's challenge)

The Krew and Warband features need friends in your group who run the toolkit too.
Some cards ask a question, like "Hold the grudge?". Click to answer yes, or right-click for no. With a controller, press Select (View on Xbox, Create on PlayStation) on the card, then A for yes or B for no.
The Scholar's challenge (riddles for kids) is off until a parent turns it on for a character. /tlk report shows a parent how it's going: what's mastered, what's being worked on, and the recent misses. After a wrong answer, the other answers wait a few seconds (counting down on their keys), so guessing every answer in a row doesn't work, and reloading or logging out doesn't skip the riddle or shorten the wait: the same riddle comes back, and the Owl notices. A riddle never traps you: you can still move, chat, and fight while it's up, and Later (L, or D-pad left) folds it into a little pill until you're ready. Esc and Start still open the game menu with one press, and every other key and button works as usual. /tlk report tells a parent how many riddles were put off or brought back by a reload. Stars buy wishes: a grown-up adds one with a price (/tlk wish 15 Ride the gryphon together), a kid can wish for something too (/tlk wish Get a turtle pet), and wishing for an item from a vendor or the auction house is a Shift-click (type /tlk wish, Shift-click the item, press Enter), /tlk wishes shows how close you are, and the Owl offers to ask when there are enough stars. A grown-up answers with a click, or /tlk grant 1 or /tlk deny 1, on any character of the account, or right away when grouped if the kid named them with /tlk parent First Last; a parent can make the wait longer or turn it off in the settings. For kindergarten and grade 1, riddles are read aloud as they appear, and some are only heard: listen, then pick the word. The Owl also reads quests aloud as they open (/tlk read reads the open one again), counts quest progress aloud ("Tough Wolf Meat: 3 of 8. 5 more to go!"), and says when a quest is ready to turn in.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
1. Type /console scriptErrors 1 once, so the game shows Lua errors.
2. Screenshot any error, then type /tlk debug status and screenshot that too.
3. Send them to whoever shared the addon with you, and say what you were doing.
