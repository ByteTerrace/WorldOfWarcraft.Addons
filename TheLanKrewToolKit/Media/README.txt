Put your own sounds here (.ogg or .mp3). Each is optional:

  CombatStart        when you enter combat
  ChooseYourDestiny  when you target a living enemy player
  FlawlessVictory    when your enemy player target dies and you took no hits
  FinishHim          warriors: when Execute becomes usable on an enemy player

Example: FinishHim.ogg

The game only sees files that existed when it launched, so fully restart the
game after adding or replacing a file (/reload is not enough).

Test with: /tlk sound <start|destiny|flawless|finish>

Updating the addon may replace this folder, so keep a copy of your files.
