Put your own sounds here (.ogg or .mp3), named exactly as below. Each is optional:
a sound with no file stays silent. If both exist, .ogg wins.

Variants: numbered copies such as FinishHim2.ogg up to FinishHim5.ogg add variety;
each time a sound plays, one of its files is picked at random.

The game only sees files that existed when it launched, so fully restart the
game after adding or replacing a file (/reload is not enough).

Test any sound with /tlk sound <word>, a variant with /tlk sound <word> 2,
and list them all with /tlk sound.

  File               Word        Plays when
  CombatStart        start       you enter combat

Mortal Kombat:
  ChooseYourDestiny  destiny     a living enemy player becomes your target
  TestYourMight      might       an elite, rare, boss or far-higher enemy becomes your target
  FinishHim          finish      your enemy-player target is in finishing range
  ComboPoint1 to 5   combo1..5   rogues and cat-form druids: each combo point you add
  FlawlessVictory    flawless    your enemy-player target dies and you took no hits
  Fatality           fatality    your enemy-player target dies after finish him
  SuperbVictory      superb      any other kill of your enemy-player target
  DeathTaunt         taunt       you die

Kill streaks (your killing blows on players, world PvP only):
  FirstBlood         first       your first killing blow of the session
  DoubleKill         double      2 kills, each within 10 seconds of the last
  MultiKill          multi       3
  MegaKill           mega        4
  UltraKill          ultra       5
  MonsterKill        monster     6
  LudicrousKill      ludicrous   7
  HolyShit           holy        8 or more
  KillingSpree       spree       5 kills without dying
  Rampage            rampage     10
  Dominating         dominating  15
  Unstoppable        unstoppable 20
  Godlike            godlike     25
  WickedSick         wicked      30

Updating the addon may replace this folder, so keep a copy of your files.
